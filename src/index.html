Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to the questions we get asked most often about our cleaning services and teams.

What cleaning services does Mops Inc. offer?

Mops Inc. provides specialized commercial cleaning services tailored to different properties and needs: comprehensive Commercial office and janitorial services; secure sanitization for Municipal and Gov facilities; and fast, high-standard turnovers for Hotel Takeovers and Vacation Rentals.

What geographic areas do you serve?

We are based in Belleville, Ontario, and proudly serve the entire Quinte region, including Belleville, Quinte West, and Prince Edward County.

How much do your cleaning services cost?

Because every space is unique, we do not have a standard flat-rate price list. We provide personalized quotes based on factors like square footage, the current condition of the space, cleaning frequency, and specific tasks required. Contact us today for a free walkthrough and customized quote!

What are your safety and sanitization standards?

Safety and hygiene are our top priorities. We follow strict disinfection protocols and use professional-grade, eco-friendly sanitizers. Additionally, all Mops Inc. staff members are fully vetted, bonded, insured, and trained to meet strict standards.

Why is being a Living Wage Employer important?

By paying certified living wages, we invest in our employees' health and financial well-being. This ensures lower staff turnover, higher job satisfaction, and a dedicated, motivated team that takes pride in delivering exceptional service to our clients.
